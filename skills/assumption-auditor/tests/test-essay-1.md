# Test Case 3: Policy Essay

**Category**: Essay / Opinion  
**Owner**: Member 2  
**Title**: Mandatory Algorithmic Auditing for Autonomous HR Hiring Systems

---

## Input Text

> "The rapid adoption of AI-driven resume screeners and video interview evaluation software in corporate HR departments poses a clear danger to workplace equity. To prevent systematic discrimination, federal legislation must mandate that all commercial hiring algorithms undergo third-party bias audits prior to deployment. 
> 
> Proponents of self-regulation argue that market forces will punish biased algorithms, but history demonstrates that corporate secrecy conceals algorithmic harm. Mandatory auditing frameworks—requiring algorithmic transparency, demographic parity validation, and public audit disclosures—will restore public trust and guarantee equal opportunity. 
> 
> Simply stripping protected attributes like race and gender from training datasets is insufficient; federal law must enforce rigorous counterfactual testing across all candidate evaluations."

---

## Expected Audit Targets (Internal Key for Testing)

1. **Load-Bearing Assumption**: Third-party auditing entities possess standardized, mathematically objective benchmarks for measuring "fairness" in candidate evaluation.
2. **Factual Assumption**: Stripping explicit protected attributes (race, gender) fails to eliminate bias due to proxy variables in candidate resumes.
3. **Value-Based Assumption**: Achieving demographic parity in hiring outcomes is a higher societal priority than maximizing predicted candidate job performance metrics.
4. **Minor Assumption**: Corporate legal departments will comply with public audit disclosures without withholding proprietary model weights as trade secrets.
